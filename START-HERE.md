# Start here

**What this is.** A system that turns a podcast episode into finished vertical Shorts. It picks the best clips, cuts them, adds captions and motion graphics in your own look, and checks every frame. Then it sends them to a private review app. You watch, leave notes, and approve. Approved Shorts get scheduled. Every note you give becomes a permanent rule.

**What you need.**

- A Mac. Face tracking uses Apple Vision, so it does not run on Windows or Linux.
- Claude Code, on a paid Claude plan.
- A free Supabase account (stores your Shorts) and a free Vercel account (hosts your review app). Free tiers may not hold many videos, so check their limits.
- A Buffer account for posting. Check current prices, because you may need a paid plan for your number of channels.
- Your recordings, as separate tracks if you can. Riverside works directly. Local files work too.
- Optional: an Auphonic key for cleaner voice. Check current prices.

Costs change, so check current prices on each site. No numbers are given here on purpose.

**The 3 steps.**

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open the folder in Claude Code.
3. Type `/shorts-setup` and answer the questions.

Setup builds your look from your colours, fonts and logo. You approve a preview before anything renders. After that, type `/shorts` for each new episode.

**What is not included, and why.**

- **No music, no LUT, no fonts, no logos.** These are licensed or yours. You bring your own, and you own the rights.
- **No raw research data.** The method and the numbers are here. The raw files are not.
- **No guarantee of views.** The research shows what winners do. It is correlation, not a promise. Most of it comes from tech and business shows, so test it in your niche.

**Rights.** You are responsible for every clip, photo and logo you put on screen. Fair use is not assumed.
