# One caption line or two?

Data: the 30 podcast Shorts in `../deconstruct` (27 YouTube, 3 Instagram; TikTok has no multiple). Apple Vision read the text at 2 frames per second. Pinned text was removed, then caption rows were counted per frame. The script and raw data are not included.

- All 30: a median of 79% of caption frames are one line. 18 of 30 are mostly one line. 6 are mostly two.
- Winners (10x or more, n=22): median 76% one line. 12 are mostly one line. 6 are mostly two.
- Others (under 10x, n=8): median 82% one line. None are mostly two.
- Top 10 by multiple: 7 of 10 are mostly one line. The top two (79.4x and 62.1x) are 75% and 95% one line.

Verdict: one line is the norm and it is safe (R76). Two lines are not wrong, since 6 winners use them, but they give no lift we can see.

Limit: machine row count on a small sample, not judged by eye.
