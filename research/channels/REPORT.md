# Podcast Shorts outliers: who to study

Measured: 90 public podcast channels, YouTube Shorts tab, Shorts 3 to 90 days old. 54 had 8+ Shorts and are ranked. Rank = sum of ranks on views per 1k subs, outlier rate (share of Shorts at 5x+ the channel median) and median views. Raw data is not included. These are public shows, named because they are the data. Most cover tech, business and culture. Do this again for your own niche (see `../README.md`).

Group A means studio shows. Group B means remote or two-host tech shows.

## Three worth studying if your show is remote or two-host

1. The Standup Clips (20,100 subs, rank 19): 247.8 per 1k, best in Group B. Remote webcam grid. Clips 100 to 180 s, max 32.5x.
2. Pivot (260,000 subs, rank 17): two hosts, remote, top-and-bottom split frame. 67.2 per 1k, max 12.1x.
3. My First Million (968,000 subs, rank 14): two hosts. 11.6% outliers, max 20.1x, 44 to 67 s clips. TikTok 30.4% outliers, Instagram 19.2%.

Runners-up: TBPN (rank 24, 41.5 per 1k, only 2 at 5x+), Hard Fork (rank 26, 3 at 5x+).

## Patterns (titles word for word)

- Famous name plus a story: "How Elon Musk solves problems" (20.1x), "What Palmer Luckey would build if he started over" (18.0x), "Toto Wolff turned down his job at Mercedes F1" (756.4x).
- Contrarian verdict: "Why The Most Expensive Steak Is OVERRATED" (57.5x), "Howard Marks: The S&P 500 isn't a good bet right now" (11.3x), "Brutal Reality of AI at Real Jobs" (15.3x).
- First line is a cold question: "Is Wagyu overrated?", "What did you sell for 1.8 million dollars?"
- Or a hot take: "Mac doesn't have an answer."
