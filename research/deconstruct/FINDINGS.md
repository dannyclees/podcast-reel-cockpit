# Podcast Shorts, taken apart frame by frame

31 Shorts from public channels: 27 YouTube from 14 channels, last 120 days; 3 Instagram; 1 TikTok. One sponsor ad was excluded, so n=30. "Conv" means studio and remote two-person clips, n=21. Raw data and videos are not included.

**Measured** by code: cuts, shots, faces, on-screen text, words, loudness (LUFS), music, sound effects, views. **Judged** by eye: who is on screen per shot, layout, hook, captions, ending.

## Findings for a remote two-person show

1. **Open on a question, at 0.0 s.** 7/15 of 10x+ against 0/6 of under 10x (judged, n=21).
2. **Cut 2 to 3 times per 10 s, first cut by 3 s.** 10x+: 2.26 and 3.07 s. Remote channels: 0.94 and 6.96 s. Spearman multiple against cuts 0.59 (n=21).
3. **Picture every noun.** Inserts fill 40% of 10x+ against 3% of under 10x.
4. **Do not ping-pong.** Two-person clips switch speaker 0.23 times per 10 s (n=16). Cut to the other host for questions and reactions.
5. **Show the listener.** 12/15 against 1/6. The best remote clips use a top-and-bottom split (TWiST 36.9x, Hard Fork 17.4x).
6. **Close face at most 60% of the time.** 0.43 against 0.90; Spearman -0.50.
7. **Always caption.** 21/21. Phrase (11) or word by word (8). Lower third (13) or split line (4). Keyword colour 5/21, emoji 1/30.
8. **Mix at -14 to -16 LUFS.** Remote webcam clips sit at -23.0 (n=5).
9. **Music bed optional, 16.6 dB under the voice** (n=8). 8/15 against 0/6, mixed up with channel style.
10. **About 55 s, end on a finished punchline, no call to action.** A call to action on 2 of 21.
11. **Title card optional.** 7/15 against 2/6.

## Hook patterns, word for word

**Host asks a question** (median 29.9x):
- "What do you think today UAPs are?" (All-In 33.6x)
- "How long does it take someone to spot a psychopath?" (Diary of a CEO 22.4x)
- "Which animal would terrify you the most if it suddenly developed human-level intelligence?" (Flagrant 29.9x)

**Famous-name story:**
- "Elon, he came to me and said, hey, look, we got to figure out how to sell cars online." (My First Million 79.4x)
- "Here's what I'll say Jensen's doing..." (TWiST 36.9x)
- "Have you heard the story of Elon getting his rockets from Texas to Florida?" (My First Million 25.9x)

**Contrarian claim** (wide spread):
- "Guns, in reality, weren't so significant." (Dwarkesh 17.3x)
- "We have no middle class anymore." (TBPN 14.2x)
- "So I'm extremely anti-public benchmarks." (Latent Space 1.0x)

**News drop** (weaker than in reels):
- "This week, a bankruptcy court auctioned off Spirit Airlines' internal corporate data." (Hard Fork 11.2x)
- "SpaceX had their Q3 earnings call yesterday..." (TBPN 12.1x)

## Where podcasts differ from general reel advice

- General advice often says captions are optional. Podcasts caption 29 of 30.
- General advice often favours a news hook. Here, questions win.
- General advice often says -14 LUFS. This set sits at -16.5.
- A call to action shows up on only 3 of 30.

## Limits

- Top Shorts only, and channel style is mixed in. This shows the winners' house style, not proven cause.
- Picks and medians came from public lists, with rounded views.
- TikTok n=1.
- The cut detector was right on 5 of 6 spot checks. Sound effects counts are a minimum.
