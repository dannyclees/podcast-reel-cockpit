# Cuts or transitions?

Data: 31 top podcast Shorts, scanned frame by frame at low resolution. A hard cut is one frame with a big jump. A gradual change is 3 or more frames of mid-size change between two shots. A flash is a brightness jump and back within 12 frames.

- 423 hard cuts, 114 gradual changes, 22 flashes. About 3 in 4 changes are hard cuts.
- 8 of 31 Shorts use a flash, about 1 in 4. By eye, these sit at a section change: into a B-roll wall, or into a list card.
- 12 of 31 Shorts have no gradual change and no flash.

Limit: the scan is crude. Some "gradual" changes are camera moves or fades inside a shot. They were not checked one by one.

Rule R94: hard cuts by default. A transition goes only at a section change, never between two speakers. Raw data not included.
